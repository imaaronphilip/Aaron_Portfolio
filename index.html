About Me

★ INSPACE 25 — Top 5

I'm Aaron Philip — a Product Designer, UI/UX enthusiast, and space-tech advocate based in New Delhi.

Final Year BTech in Biotechnology at Delhi Technological University (DTU), I merge my passion for design thinking with technological innovation.

I founded and led ASTRA — DTU's first-ever SpaceTech and Astronomy festival, bringing together 25,000+ participants and 25+ teams for an out-of-this-world experience. As the editor of The Zenith newsletter, I crafted compelling narratives for Team Rocketry DTU across seven acclaimed editions.

From securing a Top 5 position at INSPACE 25 to building a community-driven platform — I'm driven by curiosity, collaboration, and the pursuit of excellence.
